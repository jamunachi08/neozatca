# Privacy Policy

**NeoZatca** ("the App") is an ERPNext application provided by Neotec Integrated Solutions.

1. **Data collection:** The App runs entirely on your own Frappe/ERPNext site. Neotec Integrated Solutions does not collect, store, or transmit your financial data, invoices, or customer information.
2. **ZATCA communication:** Invoice data is sent only from your site directly to ZATCA (Fatoora) endpoints that you configure, for clearance and reporting.
3. **Credentials:** CSIDs, private keys and tokens are stored in your site's database and are never shared with NeoZatca.

Contact: support@neotec.ai
